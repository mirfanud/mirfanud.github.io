AMI-HJRC 2026

AI Meets Imagination with a Hands-on Journey in Robotics & Code

30–31 March 2026 (Monday & Tuesday) | Mardan, Pakistan

About the Event

This workshop aims to empower students and young innovators with practical skills in Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, and Coding. It bridges creativity and technology through hands-on learning and interdisciplinary collaboration.

Objectives

  • Build technical and creative skills in AI, robotics, and coding
  • Promote collaboration between design, engineering, and computing fields
  • Enhance STEM awareness in Newly Merged Districts (NMDs)
  • Support innovation and digital transformation in KP

Expected Outcomes

  • 150–200 student and researcher participants
  • Hands-on workshops and tutorials
  • Increased STEM awareness in NMDs
  • Strengthened research visibility in AI and robotics

Event Details
